The first FRIENDLY ROBOTS! coloring book began with b. floore’s nieces and nephews: a way to build a relationship through something they could share.

That family beginning is still at the heart of every book. Each one is dedicated to every child—including you.

At the end of every book, there is a personal letter from the author to his nieces and nephews. Written for them, and meant to be shared with everyone.
